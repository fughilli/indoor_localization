Stylus probe (instead of 1–3)

The fixture tracker (above) stays on the fixture; the fixture may move. Deploy led_pick. Pivot: tip on the blinking LED, rock the stylus round it, then probe each blinking LED. The stylus button drives it too.

1 · Rub the fixture surface

Fixture must not move from here until the mount is recorded. Deploy led_pick first (README). Sweep the tracker's flat base over the whole surface, many orientations.

2 · Touch the blinking LED

Put the contact point on the blinking LED and hold still 1 s (or press the tracker's button). Vary the tilt between touches.

3 · Mount and record

Attach the tracker to the fixture without moving the fixture, then record (hold still 1 s).

4 · Live

Deploy plane_slice or sphere_shell to match the shape (README), then go live: T_WF = T_WT · T_TF, /plane or /sphere streamed.
